# Alioune Badara Diouf

**R&D Engineer — Computer Vision, Embedded AI & Robotics**
PhD in Robotics · ISIR (UMR 7222), Sorbonne University — Paris, France

I build vision systems that have to work outside the lab: deep learning models trained,
quantised and deployed on constrained hardware, inside instruments that ship to real users.
I am now extending that work to **agentic AI** — LLM-driven systems that plan, call tools
and act on real data.

### What I work on

- **Detection & classification** on highly variable biological samples — YOLOX, RF-DETR, YOLOv8/v11
- **Embedded inference** — int8 post-training quantisation, ONNX Runtime, TFLite, Edge Impulse, ARM targets
- **From model to product** — acquisition, annotation, training, evaluation, deployment, commissioning

### Currently: agentic AI

Building LLM agents end to end — tool use, multi-agent orchestration, guardrails,
evaluation and deployment — with the **OpenAI Agents SDK**, **LangGraph**, **CrewAI**
and the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**.

The goal is to bring agents to the systems I already know: lab instruments,
vision pipelines and the data they produce.
Progress, labs and notes: **[agentic-ai-learning](https://github.com/AliouneDiouf/agentic-ai-learning)**

### Selected work

- **[AQUANT](https://www.planktovie.biz/shop/aquant)** — automated rotifer counter I designed end to end.
  YOLOX-M quantised to int8 on a Raspberry Pi 5, **97.6 % mAP**, real-time inference.
  Now sold by Planktovie.
- **[Automated microinjection of zebrafish embryos](https://github.com/AliouneDiouf/AutomatedInjectionOfZebrafishEmbryos)** —
  vision-guided robot that targets viable embryos, detects contact from the image and injects them.
- **[On-chip sorting of zebrafish embryos](https://github.com/AliouneDiouf/On_Chip_Sorting_Zebrafish_Embryos)** —
  YOLO detection driving microfluidic pumps in real time. Part of my PhD.
- **5 peer-reviewed publications**, including a Best Student Paper Award at IEEE MARSS 2023
  and an article in *Scientific Reports* (2025).
